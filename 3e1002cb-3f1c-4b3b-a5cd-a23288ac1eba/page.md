# Cause & Solution Essay — шаблон и шпаргалка

2026-11-12 · @someone

## План на контрольной

Эссе — 4 абзаца, около 230–270 слов. Время раздели так:

1. **5 минут — план.** Выпиши 2 причины и 2 решения, к каждому одно объяснение или пример. Каждое решение должно отвечать на свою причину.
2. **25–30 минут — пишешь** по шаблону ниже.
3. **3–5 минут — проверка** по чек-листу в конце.

Своё мнение (I think) не нужно: ты анализируешь проблему, а не споришь.

## Шаблон эссе

Подставляй своё вместо \[скобок\]. Абзац 2 отвечает на первый вопрос (Why?), абзац 3 — на второй (What could be done?).

**1. Introduction (2–3 предложения)**

Nowadays, \[перефразируй проблему из темы\]. The main reason for this is \[самая важная причина\]. However, there are a number of reasonable solutions to this problem today.

**2. Causes**

There are several reasons for this problem. Firstly, \[причина 1\]. This is because \[объяснение\]. Secondly, \[причина 2\]. As a result, \[последствие / пример\].

**3. Solutions**

There are two effective solutions to this problem. One way to tackle the problem is to \[решение 1\]. For example, \[пример\]. Another way of dealing with the problem is to \[решение 2\]. If this is done, \[результат\].

**4. Conclusion (2 предложения)**

In conclusion, \[проблема\] is mainly caused by \[причина 1\] and \[причина 2\]. However, if \[кто\] \[решение 1\] and \[решение 2\], the situation could be improved.

## Готовые фразы

| Абзац | Фразы |
| --- | --- |
| Introduction | Nowadays, more and more… · It is widely known that… · There is a widespread belief that… · The main reason for this is… · However, there are a number of reasonable solutions to this problem today. |
| Causes | There are several reasons for this problem. · Firstly, … · Secondly, … · This is because… · This happens because… · As a result, … · For example, … |
| Solutions | There are two effective solutions to this problem. · One way to tackle the problem is to… · Another way of dealing with the problem is to… · If this is done, … · This would help to… · If people had…, they would… |
| Conclusion | In conclusion, … is mainly caused by … and … · However, if …, the situation could be improved. · To sum up, … |

## Связки и лексика

| Что | Слова |
| --- | --- |
| Порядок | Firstly, Secondly, Moreover, In addition, Finally |
| Результат | As a result, Therefore, So, Because of this |
| Пример | For example, For instance, such as |
| Контраст | However, On the other hand, Although |
| Глаголы причины | cause, lead to, result in, contribute to, be responsible for |
| Глаголы решения | tackle, solve, reduce, prevent, improve, encourage, introduce, invest in |
| Кто решает | the government, local authorities, schools, teachers, parents, companies, the media, individuals |

## Готовые идеи по темам

У каждой причины есть своё решение. Идеи про интернет, деньги и слабые законы подходят почти к любой теме.

| Тема | Причины | Решения |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Air and water pollution | factories release chemicals; too many cars | strict environmental laws and fines; develop public transport |
| Young people and modern art | the Internet is more exciting; modern art is hard to understand | interactive exhibitions, virtual reality; explain art at school |
| Traffic jams | too many private cars; poor public transport | higher parking fees; more buses and metro lines |
| Childhood obesity | fast food is cheap; children don't move enough | healthy school meals, tax on junk food; more sport at school |
| Youth unemployment | no work experience; skills don't match jobs | internships; practical courses at universities |
| Young people read less | social media and games; reading seems boring | e-books and audiobooks; reading clubs at school |
| Stress at work | long working hours; fear of losing the job | flexible hours, remote work; sport and rest at work |
| Crime in cities | poverty and unemployment; few police on streets | create jobs and help poor families; more police and cameras |
| Plastic in the oceans | single-use plastic; people don't recycle | ban plastic bags; recycling points, education campaigns |
